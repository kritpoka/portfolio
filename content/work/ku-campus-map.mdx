<Section n="07" title="Build highlights">

- **Tappable landmarks.** Selecting a building in 3D opens its details and syncs with search. Pins are real HTML buttons positioned over the 3D scene, so Thai labels render properly and keyboard and screen-reader users can reach them.
- **Shuttle routes.** Routes drawn on the campus so “how do I get there” has an answer.
- **Performance on phones.** The scene renders on demand — a still map draws no frames — and loads as its own chunk after the app shell. <Ph>[METRIC — fps / load time on a mid-range phone]</Ph>
